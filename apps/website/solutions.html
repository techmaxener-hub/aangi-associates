Term, Endowment, or ULIP — which one, and why.

Every rupee of premium can only do one of two jobs: protect, or grow. These three products split that trade-off differently.

Term Plan Endowment Plan ULIP
Primary purpose Pure life cover Life cover + guaranteed savings Life cover + market-linked investment
Premium for the same cover Lowest Higher Higher
Maturity benefit None — pure protection Guaranteed sum assured + bonus Market-linked fund value
Returns Not applicable Modest, guaranteed Variable — market-linked, can be higher or lower
Liquidity None until a claim Limited, after a lock-in Partial withdrawal after the 5-year lock-in
Charges Minimal Built into the premium Fund management, mortality & admin charges
Best for Maximum cover at the lowest cost Disciplined, guaranteed saving alongside cover Market-linked growth, comfortable with risk

Illustrative comparison of how these product types typically work — exact terms, charges, and returns vary by insurer and plan. Talk to us for specifics on any product mentioned here.
